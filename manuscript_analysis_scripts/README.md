# Dataset

The data generated during this study are publicly available in Zenodo.

The released S1-GRiTS data products are provided as five dataset components:

(1) Gridded monthly descending-pass Sentinel-1 backscatter composites and a mosaic for mainland Ecuador, January 2026, available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20607389;

(2) Ascending-pass Sentinel-1 gridded time series for MGRS tile 17MPU, Ecuador, 2017–2025, available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20589543;

(3) Descending-pass Sentinel-1 gridded time series for MGRS tile 17MPU, Ecuador, 2017–2025, available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20607919;

(4) Multi-region gridded monthly Sentinel-1 composite products for spatial scalability demonstration, available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20604391; and

(5) Pixel-level orbit-pair statistics and static geometry variables for MGRS tile 17MPU, Ecuador, 2017–2025, available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20607604. The datasets are openly accessible without embargo or login requirements.
